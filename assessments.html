How an assessment runs

Most assessments work from exports of the tools you already use, so we rarely need standing access to your systems. Where live collection is useful, it happens only within a signed, time-boxed scope.

  1. ScopeAgree in writing on what's in scope, how evidence is collected, and when.
  2. CollectGather exports from your admin consoles and tools, or run approved, time-boxed collection.
  3. AnalyzeCorrelate devices, accounts, and findings across sources, and flag where sources disagree.
  4. PrioritizeRank issues by real-world risk and business impact, not just scanner severity.
  5. ReportWalk you through findings, the evidence behind them, and a prioritized plan.

What you receive

Deliverables written for two audiences: the people who approve the budget and the people who do the work.

  • Executive brief. A short, plain-language summary of where you stand and what needs a decision.
  • Detailed findings. Each one traced to the exact evidence it came from, so it can be verified and re-checked.
  • Prioritized remediation plan. Owners, due dates, and milestones in a format your team can track.
  • Clear unknowns. Where the evidence was missing or incomplete, we say so, and say what would answer it.
  • A walkthrough. A working session to go through results and answer questions.

Common questions

Is this a penetration test?

No. An assessment reviews configuration and evidence to find weaknesses across your environment. It doesn't try to exploit them. If you need a penetration test, we can help you scope one.

Do you need administrator access to our systems?

Usually not. Most of the work uses exports from the consoles you already have. Any live access is read-only where possible, limited to the agreed scope, and time-boxed.

How do you handle our data?

Each client's evidence is kept separately and only for the engagement's agreed retention period. Original files are preserved unchanged so every finding can be traced back to its source.

Can the results feed into a compliance program?

Yes. Assessment evidence maps directly into our GRC advisory work, including CIS Controls evaluations and remediation tracking.
