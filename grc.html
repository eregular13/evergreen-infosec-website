Trace GRC: honest results, every time

Trace GRC is the platform we built to run our evaluations. It doesn't give you a single compliance percentage. Each safeguard gets one of four results, so you always know what's proven and what isn't.

Pass

Every check passed and, where needed, a person has confirmed it with evidence.

Fail

The evidence shows a specific gap, such as a device reporting that its disk isn't encrypted.

Unknown

Evidence is missing, incomplete, out of date, or contradictory. It needs investigating, not assuming.

Needs attestation

No data can decide it, so an owner confirms it with a documented rationale and supporting evidence.

Why it matters: a percentage can look healthy while hiding the gaps that matter. Showing every unknown keeps your plan, and your answers to auditors, grounded in fact.

Built for evidence you can defend

Every result in Trace GRC traces back to the exact file, row, and column it came from. When an auditor, insurer, or customer asks “how do you know?”, you can show them.

  • Exports, not agents. Evidence comes from exports of the tools you already use, such as Intune, Defender, Entra ID, and Meraki. Nothing extra to install.
  • Tamper-evident records. Original files are kept unchanged and checksummed, and each evaluation is recorded with a date so you can show progress over time.
  • Auditor-ready packages. Export an evidence package an assessor can verify independently, without access to our systems.
  • People make the decisions. Human assessments need a rationale and evidence, and they can't override a failed check.
  • AI is off by default. AI-assisted drafting is only used if you choose to turn it on, and every draft still needs a person's approval.
  • Your data stays separate. Each client's evidence is kept in its own separate instance, never mixed with another client's.

Common questions

Can I buy Trace GRC as software?

Trace GRC is delivered as part of our advisory engagements, run by an Evergreen InfoSec consultant. You get the results and evidence packages without having to operate another platform.

Why start with CIS Controls IG1?

IG1 is the essential baseline of safeguards every organization should meet, and it lines up well with what cyber insurers and many frameworks ask about. It's the most practical place to start, and the evidence carries over as you mature.

Will you certify us as compliant?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who offers to without a formal audit. We help you get ready, close gaps, and assemble evidence so a formal audit or questionnaire goes smoothly.

Do you work with our MSP?

Yes, and we'd encourage it. They own day-to-day operations; we provide the independent evaluation and the plan.
