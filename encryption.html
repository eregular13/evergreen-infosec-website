Common questions

Will encryption slow down our computers?

On modern hardware, built-in encryption uses processor acceleration and the impact is negligible for everyday work.

What happens if someone forgets their PIN or a device won't boot?

That's what key escrow and the recovery runbook are for. Authorized staff can retrieve the recovery key and get the user working again. We test this before rollout, not after.

Do we need to buy new software?

Usually not. BitLocker, FileVault, and LUKS are built into the operating system, and we manage them through tools you likely already own, such as Intune or your MDM.

Does encryption help with breach notification rules?

Many data breach laws and regulations treat properly encrypted data differently from unencrypted data. Your legal counsel can tell you how that applies to you; we make sure you have the evidence to show encryption was in place.
