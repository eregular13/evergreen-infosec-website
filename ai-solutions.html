What forward deployment means

Not a slide deck and a handoff. We work alongside your staff, inside your environment, until the solution is running and your team owns it.

Use-case discovery

Workshops with the people doing the work to find tasks where AI saves real time, and to rule out the ones where it doesn't.

Secure deployment

Set up AI tools and assistants with the right access controls, data boundaries, logging, and identity integration for your environment.

Integration & automation

Connect AI to the systems your team already uses, such as documents, ticketing, and line-of-business tools, with least-privilege access.

AI governance

Acceptable-use policy, data classification rules, vendor review, and an AI risk register that plugs into your existing GRC program.

Testing & evaluation

Check outputs against your real tasks before rollout, and test for data leakage and prompt-injection risks.

Adoption & ongoing support

Hands-on training, office hours, and continued help as your team's use grows, so the tools actually get used well.

From idea to supported solution

Every AI engagement follows the same scoped, evidence-based approach we use for security work. You always know what's being built, what it can access, and how it's performing.

  1. DiscoverMap workflows and pick the use cases with the clearest value and lowest risk.
  2. DesignDecide what data the solution can see, who can use it, and how its output is checked.
  3. DeployBuild and roll out in your environment, starting with a pilot group.
  4. EvaluateMeasure results against the original goal and fix what isn't working.
  5. SupportTrain your team, hand over documentation, and stay available as needs change.

Security built in, not bolted on

We're a security firm first. Every AI solution we deploy gets the same scrutiny we'd give any system that touches your data.

  • Your data stays yours. Clear rules on what data AI can access, where it's processed, and whether vendors may train on it.
  • Least-privilege access. AI tools get only the permissions they need, tied to your existing identity system.
  • People stay in charge. Human review where outputs matter, so AI drafts and people decide.
  • Documented for auditors. Policies, risk decisions, and configurations recorded so you can answer customer and insurer questions about AI.

Common questions

Is this the same as OpenAI's OpenWorks?

No. OpenWorks is OpenAI's program that funds free AI coaching for eligible small businesses. We're an independent security firm, not affiliated with OpenAI. Our work is similar in spirit, hands-on and alongside your team, with security and governance built in.

We're in the OpenWorks pilot. Can you still help?

Yes. The two fit together well. Your OpenWorks Partner helps you get a first project working; we can review how it's set up, and support you once the free period ends.

Do you only work with OpenAI tools?

No. We work with the AI tools that fit your business and the systems you already run, and we'll tell you plainly when a different tool is the better choice.

We're not sure where AI fits for us. Is that too early?

No, that's the best time to start. Discovery is the first step, and a clear “not yet” for some ideas is a useful result.

Do you build custom AI models?

Usually that isn't needed. Most value comes from deploying proven AI tools well: connecting them to your data safely, setting the right controls, and helping people use them.

Our staff are already using AI tools on their own. Can you help?

Yes. We can find out what's in use, assess the risk, and give your team approved, secure options with a clear acceptable-use policy.

Do you work with our IT team or MSP?

Yes. Forward deployment means working inside your existing team, and we hand over documentation so your IT staff or MSP can run things day to day.
